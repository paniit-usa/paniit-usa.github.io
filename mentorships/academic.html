Academic Mentorships

Mentoring for academics of IIT origin (or not).

The Success in Academia Mentoring Program

This program is intended for alumni who wish to pursue a career in academia and want to learn to succeed at all levels:

  • Students at the undergrad, graduate, and postdoctoral levels
  • Assistant professors who are aiming to achieve tenure
  • Tenured faculty who want to be full Professor, Dept Chair, Dean, Provost, or President

Two cohorts: join one or both

Welcome to our mentoring sessions, where 5–10 mentees and mentors will share how they learned the ropes and climbed the ladder, or plan to. Mentees are invited to both groups.

Junior Group

Students and untenured faculty.

Senior Group

All faculty and administrators.

Fewer slides, more stories! Sharing and listening to these stories could be invaluable to you for a successful career in academia.

Planned Sessions for the Junior Group

There will be 7 sessions, one per week. During these, you will:

  1. Introduce yourself, and share career aspirations.
  2. Describe your short term as well as long term goals.
  3. Describe or ask about the culture in your academic field.
  4. Discuss cultivating connections and learning the path.
  5. Consider adapting to realities and how to change.
  6. Speed bumps you might encounter and how to manage them.
  7. Thinking long term and achieving final goals.

Needless to add, the focus here is not on content specific to your academic field, but rather on the intangibles that are critical for success.

Planned Sessions for the Senior Group

There will be 7 sessions, one per week. During these we will discuss, with examples:

Mid-March to early May
  1. The leap from academics to academic leadership: why and when.
  2. A change in values or valued change: discover your own values.
  3. Interviewing for academic leadership.
  4. Discuss cultivating connections and learning the path.
  5. Consider adapting to realities and how to change.
  6. The ladder from Prof to Chair to Dean to Executive.
  7. Leadership in Academia: distinctive features.
