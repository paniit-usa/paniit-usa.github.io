About Rethinking PANIIT

An extraordinary network deserves an extraordinary institution.

Why we started

More than a website redesign

Across generations, industries, and continents, IIT alumni are a tremendous reservoir of talent and experience. Rethinking PANIIT is an organizational transformation to help that network realize its potential — in brand, engagement, digital platforms, chapters, events, philanthropy, and connection with younger alumni.

How it works

Open, iterative, collective

  • Understand and assess before deciding.
  • Start with a few foundational initiatives.
  • Invite ideas and constructive criticism from everyone.
  • Pilot, measure, and scale what works.
