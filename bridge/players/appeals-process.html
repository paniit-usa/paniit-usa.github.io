Appeals Process

If you believe a director's ruling was wrong, you may ask for it to be reviewed.

How to appeal

  1. Ask the director to explain the ruling first. Many concerns are resolved this way.
  2. If you still disagree, tell the director you wish to appeal, ideally before the end of the session.
  3. Briefly describe the board, the ruling and why you disagree.
  4. The Appeals Committee will review it and share its decision.
