Club Rules

Rules and etiquette for PANIIT Ultimate Bridge (PUB), designed to keep our games fair, friendly and enjoyable for everyone.

Who can play

Participation is open to PANIIT alumni and their partners. All players must be registered with a valid BBO username before the start of the tournament. Playing under someone else's account is not permitted.

Tournament rules

  1. Declare your system. At the start of each table, both pairs state their bidding system and carding in the chat (e.g., "Standard American, 2/1, UDCA"). Keep your BBO profile up to date and accurate.
  2. Alert all artificial and uncommon bids, and explain them clearly when asked. This includes transfers, forcing passes, unusual notrump overcalls and any agreement that departs from standard. When in doubt, alert.
  3. No undos. A bid or card played stands as made. Please take your time before acting.
  4. No psychic bids. All bids should reflect the agreements in your system declaration.
  5. English only in table chat, so that all players and directors can follow the conversation.

Code of conduct

  • Treat every player, partner and director with courtesy. Rudeness, personal remarks and unsolicited commentary on others' decisions are not acceptable.
  • Greet your opponents and play in a timely manner.
  • If something seems wrong, call the director rather than arguing at the table.
  • Players who engage in unsportsmanlike behavior may be removed from the tournament.
