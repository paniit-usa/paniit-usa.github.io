Tournament Rules & Guidelines

PANIIT Ultimate Bridge (PUB)

Welcome to PANIIT Ultimate Bridge (PUB) — a tournament series for PAN IIT alumni and their partners. We are delighted to have you at the table. Please take a moment to familiarize yourself with the following rules and guidelines, which are designed to ensure a fair, enjoyable, and respectful experience for all participants.

Tournament Rules
1
Eligibility
Participation is open to PAN IIT alumni and their partners. All players must be registered with a valid BBO username prior to the start of the tournament. Playing under someone else's account is not permitted.
2
Declare Your System
At the start of each table, both pairs must state their bidding system and carding conventions in the chat (e.g., "Standard American, 2/1, UDCA"). Playing profiles on BBO should be kept up to date and reflect your actual system. This is not optional — it is a matter of fair play.
3
Alert All Artificial and Uncommon Bids
All artificial or non-natural bids must be alerted, and a clear explanation provided when asked. This includes but is not limited to: transfer bids, forcing passes, unusual notrump overcalls, and any conventional agreement that departs from standard. When in doubt, alert.
4
No Undos
Undo requests are not permitted during tournament play. A bid or card played stands as made. Please take your time before acting — misclicks are unfortunate, but granting undos creates an uneven playing field and is not allowed.
5
No Psyche Bids
Psychic bids are not allowed in this tournament. All bids should reflect the agreements stated in your system declaration. Deliberate misdirection through psyches undermines the integrity of the game and will be treated as a violation.
6
English Only at the Table
All table chat must be in English. This ensures that all players and any tournament directors can follow the conversation, and prevents any perception of improper communication between partners.
7
Respect at the Table
Rudeness, personal remarks, and unsolicited commentary on another player's decisions are not acceptable under any circumstances. This is a community tournament — please treat every participant with the courtesy you would expect in return. Players who engage in unsportsmanlike behavior may be removed from the tournament.
