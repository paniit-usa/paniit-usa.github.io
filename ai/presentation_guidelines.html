AI Talk Guidelines

What to expect and how to prepare for a PANIIT AI talk or webinar.

1
Keep the talk AI-focused.
Every talk should have a clear and substantial focus on artificial intelligence.
2
Connect AI to your field.
Whenever possible, focus on the state of the art at the intersection of AI and your field, discipline, or area of expertise. We are particularly interested in how AI is changing the field today and where it may be headed.
3
Make it educational, not promotional.
You are welcome to include one slide/page about yourself and your company or organization so the audience understands your background and perspective. However, the presentation should not come across as a marketing or product-promotion presentation.
4
Aim for about 40 minutes of content.
For a one-hour session, we have found that approximately 40 minutes of presentation followed by 15 minutes of Q&A works particularly well.
5
Save questions for the end.
In general, we ask attendees to hold their questions during the presentation and bring them to the Q&A at the end. This helps the speaker maintain the flow of the presentation and allows for a more productive discussion.
6
Plan for the talk to be recorded.
All talks are video recorded and will be uploaded to our YouTube channel. Please keep this in mind when preparing your presentation and demonstrations.
7
Share your slides after the presentation.
We ask that speakers share a copy of their presentation slides after the talk. A PDF version is perfectly fine.

What are the main focus areas for topics?

We maintain a balanced curriculum split between technical foundations and real-world execution:

🔷 Core AI & Machine Learning
Deep technical talks on algorithms, RL, LLM architectures, fine-tuning, RAG, agentic workflows, and model optimization.
🟧 AI Applications & Industry Use Cases
Applied AI in health, finance, product development, enterprise deployments, startup lessons, and business tools.

Who makes up the audience? Are they AI experts?

The audience is a dynamic, highly engaged mix of IIT alumni across various domain career tracks:

🖥️ AI Practitioners & Researchers
Engineers seeking technical depth, algorithmic insights, and best practices.
🚀 Tech Founders & Entrepreneurs
Leaders looking to deploy AI tools, build ventures, or discover commercial use cases.
🎓 General Alumni Junta
Tech-savvy alumni eager to keep pace with broader AI developments.
